# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-03-17

- Cited empirical research validating the four-level spectrum approach:
  - He, Houde & Weisz (CHI '25): attribution perceptions in human-AI
    co-creation — people naturally assign different credit levels by
    contribution type, amount, and initiative
  - Anon. (FAccT '26, under review): granular disclosure reduces stigma
    compared to binary acknowledgments
- Strengthened FAQ responses on stigma and ubiquity with research evidence
- Added research validation for optional `ai-prompt-url` (prompt disclosure
  decreases author comfort)
- Added ROADMAP items for compact attribution notation (AIA format) and
  IBM AI Attribution team engagement

## 0.1.0 — 2026-01-26

Initial explainer draft.

- Defined four disclosure values: `none`, `ai-assisted`, `ai-generated`,
  `autonomous`
- Proposed `ai-disclosure` global HTML attribute for element-level disclosure
- Proposed `<meta name="ai-disclosure">` for page-level disclosure
- Defined optional metadata attributes: `ai-model`, `ai-provider`, `ai-prompt-url`
- Included Schema.org JSON-LD integration example
- Aligned vocabulary with IETF AI-Disclosure header and IPTC Digital Source
  Type taxonomy
- Documented prior art: WHATWG #9479, IETF draft-abaris-aicdh-00, WICG #104,
  C2PA 2.2
- Added "What Counts as AI?" boundary guidance
- Added FAQ addressing common objections from community discussions
